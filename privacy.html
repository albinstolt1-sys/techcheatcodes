Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tech CheatCodes ("we", "us", or "our") manages user information when interacting with our YouTube automation and affiliate tools.

1. Information We Collect

Our automation tools interact solely with authorized Google APIs (YouTube Data API v3) via OAuth 2.0 to manage video uploads, schedule posts, and view channel analytics for accounts owned and managed by the authorized administrator.

2. How We Use Information

OAuth tokens and credentials are used strictly for:

  • Uploading automated video content to connected YouTube channels.
  • Retrieving channel statistics to monitor performance.

We do not sell, rent, or distribute any user data or credentials to third parties.

3. Data Security & Storage

All authentication tokens are stored locally in secured configuration files on our private servers. No credentials or personal identification data are shared externally.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the channel administrator.
